# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not open public issues for suspected security vulnerabilities.

Use GitHub's private reporting channel:

- https://github.com/grotlue/pnp-app/security/advisories/new

If the link is unavailable for your account, contact the maintainers directly and include:

- affected area and impact
- reproduction steps or proof of concept
- suggested remediation (if available)

We will acknowledge reports as quickly as possible and coordinate a fix and disclosure timeline.
